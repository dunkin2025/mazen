03 · Method

The MA-ZEN
Four-Step Method

Silence Audit → Discovery → Identity → Guidelines. Each step is a deliverable. None are skipped.

01
Always free
Silence Audit
Diagnose before designing
We audit your current sonic presence — or its absence. Delivered as a structured PDF in 48 hours. Yours regardless of whether you proceed. No call required.
02
Sonic Discovery
Map the brand's interval
A structured workshop (remote or on-site) to identify the Ma interval of your brand. What does your brand not say? Where does it breathe? This becomes the generative constraint.
03
Identity Creation
Every element evaluated against three principles
All audio elements are tested against Ma (interval), Sunyata (absence), and Wu Wei (effortlessness). Elements that fail are removed. Silence is not an absence of output — it is the output.
04
Sonic Guidelines
A codified document your team owns
The guidelines PDF is designed to be implemented without us. It specifies permitted sounds, prohibited sounds, silence ratios, interval rules, and implementation notes per context.
