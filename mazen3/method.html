03 · Design Principles

The rules we never break.

Silence First

Every design decision defaults to silence. Texture is added only to serve the boundary — never to fill it. Pure sessions run without the engine.

No Performance

We do not track, score, reward, or validate behavior. Zero events is a neutral state. Non-completion does not invalidate a session.

The Boundary is the Product

The session container — its edges, duration, and structure — is the deliverable. What occurs inside it belongs to the participant. We do not own the experience.

Narration is Interference

No voice, no cue, no instruction during the session. Structure guides without speaking. Labels are for orientation — not affirmation — and they are optional.

04 · Session Architecture

Phase sequence.

Every session advances through five structured phases. Each phase has a defined duration ratio. Transitions occur without narration.

Phase Ratio Quality Design Note
Open 8% Arrival. The field becomes present. No events in first 30s. Silence establishes the container.
Settle 15% Residue clears. Attention softens. Minimal texture permitted. Motion disabled.
Hold 52% The primary body. Stillness deepens. Silence bias maximum. Event probability lowest.
Dissolve 18% Edges soften. Return becomes possible. Slight texture increase permitted. No urgency cues.
Return 7% Reintegration. The session completes. Clean stop. No "soft landing" audio. Abrupt edges are valid.
