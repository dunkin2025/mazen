01 · Research Principles

We study interval,
not outcome.

What we explore

The structure of silence

How temporal spacing, acoustic absence, and perceptual framing affect attentional quality — without claiming to optimize it.

MA-ZEN investigates interval as a design material: Ma (間) in Japanese aesthetics, Sunyata in Buddhist cognition theory, Wu Wei in Taoist non-action philosophy.

What we do not claim

No therapeutic guarantees

MA-ZEN makes no clinical claims. Sessions are not medical interventions. We do not assert neurological effects, stress reduction outcomes, or wellness metrics.

Signal and speculation are labeled separately in all public-facing materials.

Evidence boundary

Signals vs. claims

A signal is an observed pattern that warrants further investigation. A claim is a verified, reproducible finding. MA-ZEN operates on signals until claims can be substantiated.

Methodology stance

Protocol-first design

Every session architecture is designed before deployment and held constant across iterations. Variables are isolated. Anecdotal evidence is logged separately from structural data.

Three traditions.

03 · Methodology Summary

How we test
what we design.

01 · Sources

Data inputs

Session logs (duration, completion, preset used), silence ratio measurements, sonic environment audits, enterprise client feedback structured via standardized brief format.

02 · Protocol

Test frameworks

Each session preset is held constant. Parameters are changed one at a time. No A/B testing without baseline. No inference from incomplete sessions.

03 · Boundaries

What we don't measure

Mood, stress, productivity — we do not track subjective outcomes. The session ledger is an archive, not a progress tracker. Completion is not achievement.

04 · Evidence Distilled

Key insights,
without the hype.

Most sonic branding fills space. MA-ZEN structures it. The interval between sounds is not dead air — it is the architecture your audience inhabits.

MA-ZEN · Sonic Identity Principle · 2024

01 · Silence ratio matters more than content

Sessions with silence bias above 80% produce a qualitatively different perceptual environment than those below it — regardless of the nature of the audio events present. The ratio, not the event, is the primary variable.

02 · Structure guides without narrating

Phase sequencing (Open → Settle → Hold → Dissolve → Return) provides temporal orientation without verbal instruction. The architecture is the guidance. Narration would be interference.

03 · Validity is not conditional on outcome

A session where nothing "happens" is valid. Zero events does not indicate failure. The session container is the deliverable. What occurs inside it belongs to the participant.

04 · Sonic identity operates at the interval level

In B2B audio branding, the space between sounds encodes as much brand meaning as the sounds themselves. Competitive analysis consistently reveals unexamined silence ratios — gaps in sonic identity that brands do not audit.
