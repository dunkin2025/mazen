Privacy Policy.

MA-ZEN Studio ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what we do not collect.

What We Collect

When you use MA-ZEN sessions (THE PROOF), the following data is stored locally in your browser only:

  • Session index number
  • Preset used (Ma, Sunyata, Wu Wei)
  • Session duration completed
  • Session timestamp

This data never leaves your browser. It is not transmitted to MA-ZEN servers, not used for behavioral analysis, and not shared with third parties.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Mood, stress, or wellbeing metrics
  • Engagement scores or completion rates for behavioral purposes
  • Personal health information
  • Device fingerprints or tracking identifiers
  • Location data
  • Behavioral advertising signals

Contact Form Data

When you submit a contact inquiry, we collect your company name, contact name, and email address. This data is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to mailing lists, initiate follow-up sequences, or share your information with third parties.

Analytics

We may use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics (no cookies, no fingerprinting) to understand aggregate page traffic. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

Data Retention

Session ledger data is retained in your browser until you clear your browser storage. Contact inquiry data is retained only as long as necessary to address the inquiry.

Your Rights

You may clear your session ledger at any time by clearing your browser's local storage. For contact inquiry data deletion requests, email studio@ma-zen.com.

Cookies & Tracking.

MA-ZEN Studio takes a minimal approach to cookies and tracking. Here is what we use and what we don't.

What We Use

  • Browser localStorage — for session ledger storage only (stays on your device)
  • No third-party advertising cookies
  • No cross-site tracking identifiers
  • No social media pixels
  • No retargeting mechanisms

Analytics

If we use analytics, it is cookieless and does not collect personally identifiable information. Aggregate, anonymized traffic data only — no behavioral profiling.

Third-Party Embeds

MA-ZEN sessions are self-contained and do not embed third-party scripts that track users. Font loading may reach Google Fonts servers — this is the extent of third-party network requests.

Your Control

Clear your browser's localStorage at any time to remove session ledger data. No other persistent data is set by MA-ZEN on your device.
