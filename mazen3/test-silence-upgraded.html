Method (modular breakdown)
This page’s “Method” is engineered as small modules (clear boundaries, minimal coupling).
Module What it does (concise)
01 · Consent & InitCreates AudioContext only after a user gesture; avoids autoplay failures and makes state visible.
02 · Floor SynthGenerates a looping noise bed and filters it into a neutral “room tone” field (low‑entropy baseline).
03 · Gap GateMutes the floor during structural gaps with short anti‑click fades; makes resets explicit.
04 · Marker SynthProduces non‑musical micro‑events (filtered noise + ultra‑soft edge) for orientation without melody.
05 · Timeline SchedulerSchedules marker timeouts against the protocol clock; cancels reliably on pause/stop/jump.
06 · State MachinePlay/Pause/Stop/Jump logic maintains time mapping + updates UI consistently (visibility of system status).
07 · Safety LayerSoft limiter + optional loudness clamp (“safe loudness”) to prevent spikes and support groggy use.
08 · VisualizationDraws energy bar + marker hits from analyser/time data; supports recognition rather than recall.
09 · Ritual ModeFullscreen overlay with minimal UI and a clear exit; reduces stimulation while keeping control.
10 · ReportingExports a minimal local JSON report (no tracking). Useful for debugging and iteration.
