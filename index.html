What it means — not how it works

MA-ZEN is engineered around controlled absence. Most systems add guidance, prompts, rewards, and predictable loops. We do the opposite: we leave room for reality to appear.

This page describes the user experience you can expect. The enforcement mechanisms remain private by design — because impermanence collapses if the exit logic becomes performative.

Exit Without Trace

MA-ZEN avoids habit traps. It does not optimize for retention. It optimizes for clarity.

If you want a soothing companion voice, predictable progress bars, or motivational streaks, you may find MA-ZEN unfamiliar. That unfamiliarity is the point.

Public Commitments

What we guarantee

No coercion. No streaks, no guilt loops, no manipulation UI.

No urgency. No alarm colors, no countdown pressure, no “limited time” prompts.

No dependency. Sessions can end cleanly. Silence is allowed.

Privacy by default. Local-first behavior wherever possible.

FAQ (public)

Is this meditation?

It can be used for meditation, deep work, or decompression. The method is neutral.

Will it guide me?

Sometimes, minimally. Often, not. MA-ZEN is designed so silence remains a valid outcome.
