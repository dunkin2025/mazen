Preset Definitions

PresetConceptBehavior
Ma (間)Japanese: negative space, interval, pause. The meaningful emptiness between events.High silence bias (85%). Long Hold phases. Warm amber. Very sparse events.
Sunyata (शून्यता)Sanskrit: emptiness, absence of inherent existence. Buddhist groundlessness.Maximum bias (92%). Near-zero events. Cool blue-white. Minimal transitions.
Wu Wei (無為)Taoist: non-action, effortless action, flowing with what is.Moderate bias (78%). Soft flowing events. Natural motion arcs. Green-amber.

Design Principles

PrincipleStatement
Silence FirstEvery design decision defaults to silence. Texture is added only to serve the boundary, never to fill it.
No PerformanceThe system does not track, score, reward, or validate behavior. Zero events is a neutral state.
Boundary is ProductThe session container — its edges, duration, and structure — is the deliverable. What occurs inside belongs to the user.
No NarrationNo voice, no cue, no instruction during the session. Structure guides without speaking.
