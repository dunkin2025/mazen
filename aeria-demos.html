Demo 04 · Original Concept

Vertical Transit
Sonic Field

Elevator & Lift Environment — AERIA

The elevator is the most psychologically compressed space in any hotel — 8 to 30 seconds of involuntary proximity, 0 to 40 floors of vertical displacement. This profile uses infrasonic sub-bass sweep (matched to floor ascent rate) paired with a single sustained tone at 432 Hz. Guests won't know why they feel calm. That is the goal.

  • Duration scales with floor count · 8–45 seconds
  • Sub-bass sweep mirrors ascent / descent velocity
  • Single held tone · 432 Hz · no harmonics added
  • Silence onset at door-open event
  • Imperceptible at normal volume · present at liminal threshold

Guestroom Wake Protocol
& Departure Seal

In-Room Sonic Identity — AERIA · Two-Phase Experience

The most intimate sonic touchpoint in any hotel stays: the moment of morning. AERIA's wake protocol replaces the conventional alarm with a 4-minute sonic emergence sequence — a gradual tonal rise from 40 Hz to 180 Hz, mimicking natural dawn-light amplitude. The Departure Seal is a 90-second in-room goodbye: a final tone that signals the stay has ended without words. Guests check out emotionally before they check out physically.

Phase A · Wake
  • 4-minute emergence sequence
  • 40→180 Hz gradual rise
  • No alarm signal · no percussion
  • Light mimicry via frequency
Phase B · Departure
  • 90-second farewell tone
  • Single harmonic resolution
  • Triggered by checkout event
  • Room returns to silence
