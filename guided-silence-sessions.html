Modular Breakdown — Guided Silence Sessions
Module Map
Module Function Design Principle
Preset Engine Defines the temporal and tonal character of a session. Each preset (Ma, Sunyata, Wu Wei) carries a unique phase sequence, bias range, and color signature. Silence has texture. Presets are containers, not styles.
Phase Sequencer Advances the session through structured phases (Open, Settle, Hold, Dissolve, Return). Each phase has a defined duration ratio and transitions without narration. Time structures experience. No guidance is itself guidance.
Silence Bias Meter A live readout (0–100%) of the current session's disposition toward pure silence vs. texture activity. Influenced by preset and texture parameters. The ratio of silence to event is the practice, made visible.
Texture Engine Generates sparse, canvas-rendered visual events based on Warmth and Motion parameters. All events are subordinate to silence (bias-weighted probability). Texture supports the boundary. Silence is the primary field.
Label Layer Phase name, mode tag, and bias readout. Can be disabled for pure, unmediated experience. Persists across sessions until toggled. Labels are for orientation, not affirmation. They are optional.
Ritual Mode Fullscreen, control-stripped presentation. All structural chrome disappears. Only the field and the clock remain. Activated and exited via keyboard (Escape). The frame is also content. Removing it changes the session.
Session Ledger Persistent record of session index, preset used, duration completed, and timestamp. Displayed in sidebar. Serves as archive, not progress tracker. Completion is not achievement. The record witnesses, not judges.
Validity Engine Session is marked valid at the moment it begins. Zero events does not invalidate. Non-completion does not invalidate. Validity is ontological, not conditional. The session is valid even if nothing "happens."
Preset Definitions
PresetConceptBehavior
Ma (間) Japanese: negative space, interval, pause. The meaningful emptiness between events. High silence bias (85%). Long Hold phases. Warm amber tone. Very sparse events separated by long intervals.
Sunyata (शून्यता) Sanskrit: emptiness, the absence of inherent existence. Buddhist concept of groundlessness. Maximum silence bias (92%). Near-zero events. Cool blue-white tone. Minimal phase transitions. Field dominates completely.
Wu Wei (無為) Taoist: non-action, effortless action, flowing with what is. Not passivity but alignment. Moderate bias (78%). Soft flowing events. Natural motion arcs. Green-amber tone. Events emerge and dissolve without peaks.

Design Principles
PrincipleStatement
Silence FirstEvery design decision defaults to silence. Texture is added only to serve the boundary, never to fill it.
No PerformanceThe system does not track, score, reward, or validate behavior. Events counter reads 0 as a neutral state.
The Boundary is the ProductThe session container — its edges, duration, and structure — is the deliverable. What occurs inside it is the user's.
Narration is InterferenceNo voice, no cue, no instruction during the session. Structure guides without speaking.
