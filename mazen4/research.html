MA-ZEN operates from a position of epistemic restraint. We present evidence as signal, not proof. We draw from cognitive science, psychoacoustics, and contemplative traditions — and we are explicit about the limits of each.

01 · Principles

Research Stance

Our research is structured around three epistemic commitments. None of our products make medical claims. All signal references are cited or attributed to published literature.

Commitment I

Signal, Not Proof

We distinguish between observed associations and established causality. MA-ZEN identifies patterns worth studying — not outcomes worth promising.

Commitment II

Boundary Clarity

We are explicit when we reach the edge of current evidence. Silence is not wellness. MA-ZEN does not treat, cure, or diagnose anything.

Commitment III

Reproducible Frame

Our protocol methodology is documented such that an external researcher could replicate the structural conditions of any MA-ZEN session architecture.

02 · Domain Sources

Where the Evidence Comes From

MA-ZEN draws on four research traditions. Each informs different aspects of the design — and carries different levels of evidentiary strength.

01

Psychoacoustics

The study of how the auditory system processes sound — and silence. Research from this field informs our silence-ratio design and the structuring of interval lengths. Key signal: absence of sound is not processed as neutral; it carries its own perceptual signature.

Evidentiary strength: Moderate–High Peer-reviewed literature
02

Default Mode Network Research

Neuroimaging studies on resting-state brain activity show that structured, unprompted rest periods activate the DMN — associated with self-referential thought and consolidation. MA-ZEN sessions are not designed to activate or suppress the DMN; they simply avoid interfering with it.

Evidentiary strength: Moderate fMRI literature
03

Contemplative Traditions

Japanese Ma (間), Sanskrit Sunyata (शून्यता), and Taoist Wu Wei (無為) provide structural metaphors that inform the philosophical frame of the product. These are not treated as scientific evidence — they are acknowledged as generative constraints on the design logic.

Evidentiary strength: Philosophical Not empirical
04

Sonic Branding Research

Emerging literature on audio identity, brand recall via sonic cues, and the psychophysiological effects of ambient audio on behavior informs THE CASH B2B product. This domain is young; we are explicit about the limits of current evidence.

Evidentiary strength: Low–Moderate Emerging field

03 · Methodology

How Sessions Are Structured

Every MA-ZEN session is governed by a five-phase architecture with defined duration ratios. The structure is reproducible and documented. The experience within the structure is not scripted.

Phase Duration Ratio Function Design Rationale
Open 8% Arrival — field becomes present Attentional reorientation requires a defined threshold event, not a gradual fade
Settle 15% Residue clears, attention softens Transition from task-mode requires active buffer; this phase provides it without narration
Hold 52% Primary field — stillness deepens The majority of the session is structurally unmediated. This is the product.
Dissolve 18% Edges soften, return becomes possible Abrupt return from deep stillness to activity creates jarring discontinuity; dissolve is structural, not therapeutic
Return 7% Reintegration — session completes The session must have a defined terminal edge. Soft endings remove the structure that makes the interval meaningful.

04 · What We Don't Claim

Evidence Boundaries

Explicit boundary-setting is part of our research integrity posture. The following claims are ones MA-ZEN deliberately does not make.

Not Claimed

MA-ZEN sessions reduce stress or anxiety

What We Say Instead

Structured silence removes certain categories of auditory stressor for the duration of the session. What a user does with that absence is their own.

Not Claimed

MA-ZEN improves focus or productivity

What We Say Instead

The absence of interruption and prompting within a defined session period creates structural conditions that differ from ambient media. Whether this is useful is user-determined.

Not Claimed

MA-ZEN is meditation

What We Say Instead

MA-ZEN is structured silence. Meditation is a practice with a tradition, a teacher, and a goal. MA-ZEN has a session structure, a silence ratio, and an interval. These are different things.

The restraint of the claim is itself a product feature. A system that does not promise transformation cannot disappoint by failing to deliver it.
